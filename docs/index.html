Executive overview

What this project does

Built by the SCUI Big Data team, this repository turns the Yelp Open Dataset into reproducible analytics: domain notebooks, PySpark scripts, and publication-ready CSV + chart artifacts under results/. Work spans two tracks - core data analysis and external data enrichment - so findings sit in real-world context, not just platform metrics.

Selected results

Highlights from the analyses

A sample of publication findings exported from module notebooks into results/.

Market structure

Starbucks (724) and McDonald’s (703) lead chain density. Philadelphia tops city merchant counts (~14.5k). Restaurants, Food, and Shopping dominate category volume.

Weather mood

Extreme heat posts the lowest average rating (~3.75) with elevated 1-star share. Heavy snow cuts foot traffic by roughly 80% versus normal conditions.

Trust & manipulation

260 businesses flagged for anomalous 5-star spikes. Among suspicious reviews, over half come from zero-fan accounts; persuasive tokens like “definitely” and “recommend” dominate fraud-linked text.

Review language

Useful votes exceed 8.2M platform-wide. Pain-point bigrams recur around wait times and wasted money. 1-star reviews run longer (~134 words) than 5-star reviews (~85).

Location risk

Walkability and closure patterns surface “potentially cursed” and “severely cursed” storefront cohorts, with pain points clustering around bad location and price issues.

Crime context

Restaurant survival and engagement differ across high/medium crime zones; zone-level rating, cuisine, and check-in tables support cross-metric comparison.

Runtime

Technology & workflow

Processing runs on Apache Spark (PySpark) with Apache Zeppelin notebooks (%pyspark). Queries use Spark SQL / DataFrame APIs against Hive-compatible catalogs such as yelp_db.*, yelp.*, and external_data.*.

  1. Explore Open domain notebooks under notebooks/.
  2. Execute Run matching PySpark scripts in src/ once catalog tables are available.
  3. Publish Export CSV and graph artifacts to results/ for reporting and QA review.
